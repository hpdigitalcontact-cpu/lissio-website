Before you ask06 / 06

The questions every owner asks us.

Who is Field Pilot built for?{{ faq0.sign }}

Owner-operated lawn care and landscaping companies in the US, from a solo crew to ten. Not plumbers, not cleaners — which is why there's nothing on screen you don't use.

Can it handle recurring jobs and multiple crews?{{ faq1.sign }}

Yes. Set a property to weekly, bi-weekly or monthly and the visits generate themselves. Assign each crew its own route, and see all crews side by side for the day.

Is the lawn care app really free for my crew?{{ faq2.sign }}

Yes. You pay for your plan; every crew member you invite gets the iOS or Android app at no cost, however many you have.

My crew isn't good with technology.{{ faq3.sign }}

One button in the morning, tick the tasks, take photos. No menus, no training session — most crews are running it the same day.

What if there's no signal at the property?{{ faq4.sign }}

The app works offline. Tasks, photos and timings sync to your back office the moment the truck gets signal again.

How long until I'm actually running on it?{{ faq5.sign }}

Add your customers and crews and your first route is scheduled in under 10 minutes. Recurring visits then generate themselves.

Do I still need accounting software?{{ faq6.sign }}

For quotes, invoices and getting paid, no — Field Pilot handles it from completed visits. Your accountant still gets clean exports at year end.

What is lawn care software?{{ faq7.sign }}

It's the software a lawn care or landscaping business runs on: scheduling recurring jobs, planning crew routes, keeping customer and property records, and sending quotes and invoices — instead of juggling texts, paper and spreadsheets.
